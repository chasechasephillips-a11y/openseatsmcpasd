Who can vote

If you live here and you vote here, you can vote.

The MCPASD Annual Meeting is the only place this petition gets decided. It's not on a regular ballot — it happens in the room, by a vote of MCPASD electors physically present. Under Wis. Stat. § 120.08, that means anyone who meets all of the below.

You're eligible to vote if:

  • You live inside the Middleton-Cross Plains Area School District (Middleton, Cross Plains, Westport, Springfield, and parts of Berry, Vermont, Roxbury, and Madison).
  • You are 18 or older.
  • You are a US citizen and have lived at your address for at least 28 days.
  • You are physically present at the meeting on Sept 22.
What to expect

The meeting in four steps.

It's a Wisconsin school district Annual Meeting — long-standing, simple, public. Come at 6:30 for pizza outside, and the meeting opens at 7:00 with a short district budget review (10–15 minutes) before the petition comes to the floor. Plan on being home by about 7:45.

01

Sign in

No ID needed. You sign a statement that you're an eligible elector living in the district. Find a seat.

02

Agenda + petition presented

The moderator reads the petition and explains the proposed change to the board apportionment.

03

Brief discussion

Anyone in the room can speak for or against — usually 1–2 minutes each. No filibustering allowed.

04

The vote

Show of hands or paper ballot, depending on the moderator. Simple majority of electors present decides.

Before you come

Quick tips to make it painless.

  • Join us for pizza starting at 6:30. We'll be outside before the meeting — come eat, say hi, and walk in together. Everyone's welcome, whether you've been part of this all summer or just heard about it today.
  • Get there by 6:45 at the latest. That gives you time to park, sign in and find a seat before the 7:00 start — or come at 6:30 and none of it feels rushed.
  • Bring a friend who's also an MCPASD elector. Every neighbor in the room is another vote in favor. About 30 people came in 2025; we need hundreds.
  • You don't need to bring anything. There's no ID requirement — you sign a short statement at the door that you're an eligible elector who's lived in the district at least 28 days.
  • Pre-plan parking. Free parking on site at Middleton High School, 2100 Bristol Street. Follow signs for the Performing Arts Center entrance — and note this is the high school, not the district office on South Avenue.
  • Bring your kids if that's what it takes. Children are welcome in the room. Sit near an aisle, bring headphones or a snack, and don't give bedtime logistics a veto over your vote.
  • Text three people today. Ask them directly and offer to drive — it works far better than a link. Send them here: openseatsmcpasd.org/sept22.

The petition is the price of admission. The vote is at the meeting. Only people physically in the room can vote it through.
