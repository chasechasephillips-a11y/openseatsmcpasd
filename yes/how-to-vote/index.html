How to vote: flip it over

Everything you need to vote in the Middleton-Cross Plains Area School District on Nov 3, 2026. The school question is on the back of the ballot.

Key dates

Pick your day.

  • Oct 20Tuesday

Three steps

1. Check your registration

Go to myvote.wi.gov and search your name. Online and mail registration close Wednesday, Oct 14. After that you can register at your clerk's office during early voting or at your polling place on Election Day, with proof of residence.

2. Choose how you'll vote

Early, in person: Oct 20 through Nov 1 at your municipal clerk's office. The City of Middleton votes at City Hall, 7426 Hubbard Ave, weekdays 8:30am to 3pm and until 5pm the Friday before. The Village of Cross Plains and each town set their own hours; check myvote.wi.gov or call your clerk.
By mail: request a ballot at myvote.wi.gov by Thursday, Oct 29 at 5pm, then return it as soon as you can.
On Election Day: Tuesday, Nov 3, 7am to 8pm at your polling place.

3. Bring a photo ID and flip your ballot over

Wisconsin requires a photo ID to vote. The Middleton-Cross Plains Area School District question is on the back of the ballot. Don't stop at the front.

Who votes on this?

Everyone who lives in the school district, which covers the City of Middleton, the Village of Cross Plains, the Towns of Middleton, Cross Plains, Springfield and Berry, and parts of neighboring towns and the City of Madison. Your sample ballot on myvote.wi.gov shows whether the question is on yours.
