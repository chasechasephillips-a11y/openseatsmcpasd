Referendum FAQ: straight answers

The questions we hear most at the door about the Middleton-Cross Plains Area School District referendum on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot.

What is the MCPASD referendum on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot?

It's a four-year, non-recurring operating referendum for the Middleton-Cross Plains Area School District. It lets the district exceed the state revenue limit by $24.9M in 2027-28, $27.9M in 2028-29, $31.1M in 2029-30 and $34.6M in 2030-31. It renews the 2022 referendum, which ends after the 2026-27 school year.

Isn't this a new tax on top of the old one?

No. The 2022 referendum ends after 2026-27. This one starts the next year and takes its place. They never overlap. It replaces, it doesn't stack.

Will my property taxes go up?

Not in year one: 2027-28 holds the same $24.9M already in place. After that the amounts step up each year. The district estimates the added cost reaches about $64 a year per $100,000 of fair market value by year four. Use the tax impact page to see your home's number.

How much will the referendum cost me?

$0 more in year one. By year four, the district estimates about $64 a year per $100,000 of fair market value, or about $256 a year on a $400,000 home. That's roughly $21 a month.

What happens if the referendum fails?

The district says it would face cuts of nearly 20% of its operating budget, as many as 250 positions, and a possible elementary school closure. That means bigger classes and a harder time keeping teachers.

Didn't we just vote on this?

Yes. In 2022, 63% of voters said yes. That approval was always for four years, and it runs out after 2026-27. This vote renews it.

Why is the district asking for more than $24.9M in later years?

Year one is the same $24.9M as today. The later amounts step up to keep pace with rising costs, because the state's cap on school revenue grows only $325 per student a year, far below inflation. The amounts are set on the ballot, and it ends after 2030-31.

What does the money pay for?

Day-to-day operations: class sizes, teachers and staff pay and benefits, current programs, and upkeep of existing buildings. It's an operating referendum, not a building project.

Is the referendum for new buildings or a pool?

No. New buildings would need a separate vote. This is for operating costs.

Is this forever?

No. It covers four school years, 2027-28 through 2030-31, then ends. Voters decide again after that.

My assessment went up. Doesn't the district get more money?

No. The state caps the total the district can collect. When assessments rise, the tax rate falls and the district collects the same capped amount. Your assessment changes your slice of the pie, not the size of the pie. Only voters can make it bigger.

Can't the school board just raise the levy?

No. State law bars a district from going over its revenue limit, and the state would take back any excess by cutting aid. Only voters can approve going above the cap.

Why doesn't the state just fix school funding?

It should, and we should push it to. But the statewide general aid pot has been flat at $5.58 billion for three years, and the revenue cap grows far slower than costs. Our kids can't wait for Madison.

Isn't the district spending too much?

MCPASD spends about $17,160 per student, close to the Dane County average. Moody's rates it Aa1, its independent audit was clean, and it plans about $5 million in cuts over the next four years even if the referendum passes.

How does MCPASD's school tax rate compare to other districts?

MCPASD's 2025-26 school tax rate is $8.62 per $1,000 of property value, 4th lowest of 13 Dane County districts and below the $9.26 average, using the state's equalized rates for every district.

I don't have kids in school. Why should I vote yes?

Strong schools hold up every home's value. MCPASD earned the state's top rating, Significantly Exceeds Expectations, with its best score ever (84.4).

If the referendum fails, will my taxes go down?

Probably, for the school line of your bill. So would nearly 20% of the schools' operating budget. It's an honest trade: a smaller bill and much smaller schools.

I'm on a fixed income. Is there help?

Year one adds $0. Wisconsin's Homestead Credit and the lottery and gaming credit lower property taxes for many homeowners. Ask your clerk or a tax preparer.

Where is the referendum on the ballot?

On the back. Flip your ballot over and look for the Middleton-Cross Plains Area School District question.

When can I vote?

Early in-person voting at your municipal clerk's office starts Tuesday, Oct 20 and ends Sunday, Nov 1 in most places. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov 3, 7am to 8pm. Bring a photo ID. The deadline to register online is Oct 14; you can still register in person after that.

Who is behind this website?

The MCPASD Yes Committee: parents, retirees and neighbors volunteering their time. The school district can share facts, but it can't ask you to vote yes.

Where can I check the numbers myself?

The district's referendum page has the official facts and a tax calculator. Your own tax bill details are on AccessDane.
