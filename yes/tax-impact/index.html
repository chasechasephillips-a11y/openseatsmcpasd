Why your bill can rise without the schools getting more

The state caps what MCPASD can collect from state aid and property taxes combined. When home values rise, the tax rate falls and the district collects the same capped total. Your assessment changes your slice of the pie, not the size of the pie.

This year the state's share of that pie shrank: the district's preliminary budget shows equalization aid down about 15%, so local property taxes covered more of the same total. The final state aid numbers come out Oct 15. More on why we're voting again.
