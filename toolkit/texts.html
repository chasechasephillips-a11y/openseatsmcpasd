Someone on the fence about the idea

Quick context since I know this stuff can sound political: it isn't. Board stays at 9 seats, nobody loses a seat, every area keeps a guaranteed representative, and voters still elect everyone. The only change is that 4 of the 9 seats could be run for by any district resident instead of only people living inside one area line drawn in 1963. Verona already works this way. Neighborhood post

Middleton / Cross Plains neighbors — one date worth knowing: the MCPASD Annual Meeting is Tuesday, September 22 at 7:00 PM at the District Services Center, 7106 South Avenue. There's a citizen petition on the agenda to open 4 of the district's 9 school board seats to any MCPASD resident. Today every seat is tied to a geographic area boundary set in 1963, so a qualified neighbor who lives a few blocks outside an area with an open seat simply can't run. The board stays at 9, every area keeps a guaranteed anchor seat, no current member loses anything, and voters still elect every seat. Under Wisconsin law this is decided by the electors physically present that night — no absentee, no proxy. About 30 people attended last year. If you'd like a say either way, that's the room to be in. Takes roughly 30 minutes, bring a photo ID. More at openseatsmcpasd.org

Three rules for all of it

  1. Individual texts beat group texts. A group chat gets read; a one-on-one gets answered. Send five individual texts before you send one group text.
  2. Ask for a specific yes. "Come with me?" converts. "Let me know if you're interested" does not.
  3. Reminders aren't nagging. Most people who don't show up wanted to and forgot. The Tuesday 5:30 text is worth more than everything you sent Wednesday.
