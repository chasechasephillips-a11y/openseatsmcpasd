Assessment door hangers

Two standard 4.25" × 11" hangers per letter sheet. The QR goes to the "did you out-jump the average?" calculator and is tagged so the site stats show hanger traffic.

  • City version prints the City's +32% figure.
  • Town version leaves the average blank because the Town did its own revaluation. Once you get the townwide % from Town Hall, write it in (or ask me to print it).
  • Print on 80–100 lb cover stock. Cut down the center line, cut out the red circle and slit. A print shop can die-cut 4.25 × 11 hangers from the PDF if you're doing hundreds.
  • Doorknobs only. Never put them in or on mailboxes; that's a federal no-no.

In the print dialog: Letter · Portrait · Margins: None · Background graphics: ON.
