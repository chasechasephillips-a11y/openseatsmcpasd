# Google Ads Playbook: Winning the Search War

**Goal:** when anyone in the district searches anything about this vote, our ad is the **first thing on the page**, and our site, Reddit thread and video fill the rest of it.

All copy below has been checked against Google's character limits (headlines ≤30, descriptions ≤90, sitelinks ≤25, callouts ≤25). Copy and paste it as-is.

---

## The 4 settings that decide who wins

Most local campaigns lose the search war in the settings, not the copy.

1. **Bid strategy: Target impression share → "Absolute top of page" → 95%**, with a max CPC limit of **$4**. This tells Google "put us first, every time," which is exactly the job. (Don't use "Maximize conversions." You have no conversion data, and 26 days isn't enough to train it.)
2. **Location option: "Presence: people in or regularly in your locations."** The default ("presence *or interest*") also shows the ad to people in Ohio reading about Middleton. Change it.
3. **Turn OFF:** Search Partners, Display Network, **auto-applied recommendations** (Google will quietly switch you to broad match), and **automatically created assets** (Google will write ad text without your "Paid for by" line).
4. **Match types: phrase and exact only.** Broad match wanders, and you don't have the budget or the time to babysit it.

## Setup checklist (do it in this order; approvals are the slow part)

- [ ] Create the Google Ads account and complete **advertiser verification** today. If Google classifies the ads as election ads, it will also ask for **election-ads verification** (an ID, and possibly committee documents). Have your CF-1 registration handy.
- [ ] Register the committee as **"Vote Yes to Renew MCPASD"** (title case). Google's editorial rules can reject ads with all-caps words like "YES," and the "Paid for by" name in your ads should match the registration exactly. The website logo can still style it as YES.
- [ ] Campaign: **Search**, English, the locations below, the 4 settings above.
- [ ] Build the 5 ad groups below, each with one responsive search ad and its keywords.
- [ ] Add the account-level negatives, sitelinks and callouts.
- [ ] Before you spend a dollar, use Google's **Ad Preview tool** (Tools → Ad Preview and Diagnosis) with the location set to Middleton.

**Bing/Microsoft Ads:** skip it. Microsoft's ad policy doesn't allow political or ballot-measure ads.

## The 10-minute daily check

1. **Search terms report:** add anything irrelevant as a negative (see the starter list below).
2. **Auction Insights:** shows who else is bidding on your terms. If a No group appears, raise the CPC limit on groups 2–4.
3. **Impression share:**
   - "Lost to rank" → raise the max CPC limit.
   - "Lost to budget" → raise the daily budget.
   - Target: **95%+ absolute top** on groups 2, 4 and 5.
4. **Click-through rate:** if a group is under 5%, swap in its strongest headline from another group.

**Starter negative list** (account level):
```
jobs  careers  employment  salary  substitute  "teacher jobs"  calendar  "lunch menu"  "snow day"
closing  closed  skyward  "infinite campus"  "bus route"  enrollment  "student registration"
"kindergarten registration"  football  basketball  tickets  scores  athletics  schedule
middletown  "middleton ma"  "middleton massachusetts"  "middleton idaho"  "middleton id"
```
Don't add a plain "registration" negative. It would block "voter registration."
