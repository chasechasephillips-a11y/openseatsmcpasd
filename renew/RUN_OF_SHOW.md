Could you confirm: (1) the authority by year ($24.9M / $27.9M / $31.1M / $34.6M); (2) the estimated tax impact per $100K of fair market value for years 1–4; (3) that year one requires no increase over this year; and (4) the link to the district's referendum FAQ? Separately, how does a resident committee file a Form CF-1 with the school board clerk? Thank you!
> [Your name]
